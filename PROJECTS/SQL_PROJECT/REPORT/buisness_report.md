Business Report
Executive Summary
During the analyzed four-month period, the company generated total revenue of 6,109.
The database contains 40 registered customers, but only 6 customers made purchases.
A total of 8 orders were completed.
The top customer was Emma Johnson, who generated 2,099 in revenue. This represents approximately 35% of total company revenue.
The Laptop category generated the highest revenue and contributed approximately 37% of total revenue.
The payment success rate was 87.5%, which means most transactions were completed successfully.
The largest order generated 1,320 in revenue.

Revenue Analysis
Revenue changed during the analyzed period. January was the most profitable month and generated approximately 50% more revenue than the average monthly revenue.
March had the lowest revenue and generated around 50% of the average monthly revenue.
In April, revenue returned to a more stable level.
The total revenue after four months reached 6,109.

Customer Analysis
Revenue is concentrated among a small number of customers. This creates a business risk because losing one or two major customers could significantly affect total revenue.
Emma Johnson generated approximately 35% of total company revenue.
Also, only 6 of 40 registered customers made purchases, which means the company has a lot of potential to acquire new customers.

Recommendation
Increase marketing activities to attract more customers.
Reduce dependence on a small number of customers.
Focus on retaining high-value customers.
Product Analysis

-The Laptop category is the strongest revenue-generating category and contributes approximately 37% of total revenue.

-The Smartphone category also shows strong potential and contributes approximately 31% of total revenue.

-The Gaming category contributes around 17% of total revenue and may provide additional business opportunities.

Recommendation
Continue investing in the Laptop category.
Expand the Smartphone category because of its strong potential.
Develop the Gaming category to increase future revenue.
Payment Analysis
The payment success rate was 87.5%, which indicates that most payments were completed successfully.
However, failed transactions still represent lost revenue opportunities.

Recommendation
-Analyze failed transactions.
-Improve payment processing.
-Monitor payment performance regularly.
-Key Findings
-Revenue is concentrated among a small number of customers.
-Emma Johnson generates approximately 35% of total revenue.
-The Laptop category is the strongest revenue driver (37%).
-The Smartphone category has strong growth potential (31%).
-Only 6 of 40 registered customers made purchases.
-The payment success rate is 87.5%.
-The company should invest in marketing to increase the customer base and diversify revenue sources.

Final Recommendation
The company should focus on increasing the number of customers through marketing activities while continuing to invest in the highest-performing categories, especially Laptops and Smartphones. This will help increase revenue and reduce business risk in the future.
